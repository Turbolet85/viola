## Goal
Every error a `viola` process gives a machine reader carries a code and a fixed message and nothing else, and the set
of those bodies is one a reader can check (per security-plan §Error Handling; architecture §Conventions, Error handling
schema). What viola builds itself already holds: every `thiserror` message is a literal, `ProtocolError`'s bodies are
pinned, and the refused documents are written from closed codes (research §Sweeps). This chunk closes what does not hold
and makes the set provable:

1. **What a client copies from its peer is screened.** Both clients copy the wire's `message` and any `data` object into
   `wrapper-fault`'s `detail`, and the wire's `refusal` and `detail` into the refusal document, into MCP
   `structuredContent` and into the text `refused: <reason>` (research §Premise closure). One shared reader replaces the
   two copies of `fault_detail`, and one code-word check screens the refusal. The wire form does not change, and `v` stays 1.
2. **The `-32602` causes are told apart** on the `channel-response` line, on both sides, in three closed words, ending the
   recorded over-count of `viola.compat.v_rejected` (per obs-plan §5).
3. **`Refusal` folds into `AgentError`**, closing that divergence the way `StampError` was closed (per architecture
   [Error Handling]). Its outputs do not move.
4. **The PREREQ's two gaps close:** the pre-`initialize` stdin cut is driven, at `serve` and at the `viola mcp` process,
   and each of the five `BOUNDARIES` rows names a case that drives past its cap (per test-plan §4 viola-mcp, §6 Contract
   suite). The masters' "exit 11" is corrected: it is the unit child's own code, and the process exits 1 (research §Premise
   closure).
5. **The proof:** the error-body check test-plan §6 names lands in `tests/support/events.rs`, a witness per surface drives
   its failures with a path, an upstream text and a planted stripped value in play, and a contract binary enumerates the
   bodies and censuses their writers.

The chunk claims `verification-matrix.json#v1-47`, its web clauses out by the founder's ruling of 2026-10-16 in the cap's
own notes. The WATCH on Path 1's CI timeout is carried and tallied on this chunk's CI reads (`inputs#I1` item 7); no step
changes Path 1's start sequence.
