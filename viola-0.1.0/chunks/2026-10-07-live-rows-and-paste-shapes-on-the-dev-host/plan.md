### Step 2 — live session 1: the scratch session (start 1)
One session, ended by Ctrl-C twice. Each text is synthetic ASCII opening `viola probe:` and is kept whole in
`evidence/scratch-prompts.json`; a long text is 1 500 bytes on one line (the tree's `PROBE_LONG_PASTE` where one
serves, printed by the helper), a short one under 200 bytes. In order, the entry's own readings first:
1. **typed-then-paste** — typed bytes, then one bracketed paste of a long text, then CR.
2. **paste-then-typed** — one bracketed paste of a long text, then typed bytes, then CR.
3. **two-pastes** — two bracketed pastes of two different long texts, then CR.
4. **long-ending-newline** — one bracketed paste of a long text whose last byte is LF, then CR.
5. **short-ending-newline** — one bracketed paste of a short text whose last byte is LF, then CR.
6. **typed-task-notification-at-start** — one short bracketed paste that opens `<task-notification>`.
7. The hold: the peer message (below).

For each of 1 to 6 the record line (`evidence/scratch-session.ndjson`, `kind` `shape`) holds the `id`, whether the
prompt arrived wrapped, the raw `prompt` (in the prompts file), what `hook::normalise` gives for it through the
helper (`origin`, and whether `text` equals the bytes sent, with both lengths), and the hint's two times where a
long paste drew it: from the paste to the input-box literal's return, and from the Stop to it. A paste that gets
no UserPromptSubmit within 60 s is recorded as that and the session goes on to its end.

**The peer message** (the founder's first answer, inputs#I3). With the scratch session idle at its hold:
- read this session's peer listing before the scratch start and at the hold. Exactly one peer must be new and
  idle; with none, or more than one, nothing is sent and the line reads `outcome` `not-listed` (a finding, which
  the founder's answer names);
- send that peer one message, once, with this fixed text and nothing else:
  `viola probe: a synthetic cross-session message and nothing else. Reply with the single word ok`;
- the `kind` `cross-session` line records the outcome, the raw start of the captured `prompt` up to the end of its
  opening tag with every attribute value replaced by a placeholder, the attribute names, `origin` as
  `hook::normalise` files it, and which of the two compiled forms the raw start matched, or `neither`;
- anything that session sends back to this one is model output of a probe: counted, never acted on.

**The names** (the founder's second answer). The `kind` `claude-names` line holds the names the scratch hook wrote,
and `outside_floor`: those not among the eleven, compared against a literal copy of the eleven typed into the
reading script (never the product's constant).

After the session: the driver's dir is gone, a `census` `after` line is written, and the raw captures stay in the
scratchpad. Only the `prompt` field and key names enter the repository: a whole payload carries host paths.

  session; none or several new peers means nothing is sent.

  address; only `prompt`, key names and bare `CLAUDE*` names enter the repository.

  §Security Anti-Patterns → Universal.

  `human-typing` one.
