## Goal
The wrapper gets a real budget gate. It reads the newest usage reading from `budget.json` at three points
(start, a `send`'s arrival, each `turn-ended`), compares it with two thresholds from `config.json`, refuses an
automated `send` with `budget-paused` while a window is over its threshold, and lifts the pause when that
window's reset passes or when the human runs `viola release <name> --budget` (per architecture §Established
Decisions [Budget Governor]; test-plan §6 Path 6; obs-plan §4 Scenario: Budget governor). It also writes the
`budget-gate` lines and the snapshot fields the log replay already reads.

Two defects in shipped code are fixed, each with its own acceptance and witness (`inputs#I1` note 3). Both were
measured at P3 (research.md, Measured at P3): `viola revive` over a group-writable home narrows the home and
refuses for a later cause, and `viola hook statusline`'s refusal of such a home lasts one invocation. Both arms
then check before they open anything under the home, and a refused check writes no log line (`inputs#I3`,
question 1).

Nothing here starts the real `claude`, adds a ledger row or a `viola verify` child, or re-stamps a home: those
are the founder's (`inputs#I1` note 2). Everything is built and rehearsed under the fake agent, and step 15
holds the operator pass until his word on the review card's items is recorded. The chunk claims no capability
(Implementation notes).

   (research.md finding 2: it does not exist). Add a closed enum of the two window words beside `HumanTyping`,
   `BudgetWindowKind` with `FiveHour` and `SevenDay`, whose `as_str` gives `five-hour` and `seven-day`. No new
   dependency: `nutype` and `serde` are `viola-core`'s two.
2. **`viola-state`: the reader and the snapshot field.**

     nothing. An absent `budget.json` returns nothing and creates no file. Otherwise it runs
     `strict::check_path` on the home and on `budget.json`, takes a shared lock on `budget.json.lock`, reads
     through `take(MAX_FRAME + 1)`, and returns nothing when a check refuses, when the bytes pass `MAX_FRAME`,
     when they do not parse, or when `v` is not 1. It never writes `budget.json`.
   - `crates/viola-state/src/snapshot.rs`: add `budget_override_until`, an optional string, additive in the
     form `cwd` has (`#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")]`); the envelope's `v` stays 1.
     Add the field at each of the 12 `InstanceSnapshot` literal sites (research.md, Graph impact: 18 hits of
     `grep -rn -E '^\s*budget_paused: ' --include=*.rs src crates tests`, 6 of them `Replayed` literals).
3. **The thresholds** (`src/run/budget.rs`, new; `src/obs.rs`). A `Thresholds` pair of `Percent`, defaults
   `five_hour` 90 and `seven_day` 85, and `read_thresholds(home)`: its own bounded read of `config.json` in the
   shape of `read_env_keep` (`src/run/env.rs:31-67`). The keys are top-level `five_hour` and `seven_day`. A
   value that is not a whole number from 0 to 100 gives that key's default and one
   `ConfigRejection::Malformed`; the other key stands; an absent, unreadable or malformed document is
   `read_diagnostics_level`'s to report, once. `CONFIG_KEYS` (`src/obs.rs:33`) gains the two names so they are
   not counted as unknown.
4. **The decision** (`src/run/budget.rs`). A pure function of the reading (or none), the thresholds, the
   override's end (or none) and the wall-clock instant, passed as an argument (research.md finding 3). Its
   rule, in order:
   - a window is *crossed* when its `used_percentage` is known and at or above its threshold, and its
     `resets_at` is known, parses as RFC 3339 and is later than the instant (`inputs#I3`, question 2: an
     `unknown` `resets_at` does not pause; a passed one has lifted);
   - with both crossed, the window named is `five-hour`;
   - an override whose end is later than the instant keeps the gate open, whichever window is crossed; one
     whose end has passed is dropped;
   - else a crossed window pauses, with its window word, percentage and `resets_at`.
5. **The gate** (`src/run/budget.rs`). One `BudgetGate` per wrapper: the last recorded state (paused with its
   window facts, or open, and the override's end) under one `Mutex`, with the instance's name, directory,
   home, thresholds and the shared `Snapshots`. `evaluate(trigger, instant)` reads `budget.json`, decides,
   and, only when the triple (paused, window, override end) differs from the last recorded one, appends one
   `budget-gate` line through the shared append (`paused`; `window` when paused; `override_until` when an
   override stands; no other key) and updates the snapshot's `budget_paused` and `budget_override_until`
   through `Snapshots::update`, inline, before it returns. It is declared
   `#[instrument(skip_all, name = "run.budget_eval", fields(trigger, paused, window))]` with `trigger` one of
   `start`, `send`, `turn-ended`. It starts no thread and holds no timer.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Opening the log after a refused check, to keep its line — rejected: the opening narrows the home, so the
  refusal lasts one invocation (research.md, runs A1, A2 and C; `inputs#I3`, question 1).
- Writing the refusal's line without narrowing the home — rejected: it writes under a home that failed the
  check, the playbook's "Boundary widening" pattern; never a plan default.
- A poll, a timer thread or a fourth evaluation point (`release --budget` evaluating) — rejected:
  `architecture.md:72` names three points and says "It never polls".
- Restoring an override at start from the prior snapshot or the replay — rejected (`inputs#I3`, question 4).
- `Percent` or the decision's clock in `viola-core` with chrono — rejected: `viola-core`'s dependencies stay
  `nutype` and `serde`.
- An environment variable, a test seam or an in-process clock to move a spawned wrapper's time — rejected: the
  expiry is a unit case; two seams exist and a third needs the founder's word.
- A hand-written `budget.json`, `snapshot.json` or `ledger/stamps.json` in any test — rejected (`testing.md`,
  Test data).
- A new `ObsEvent` value, a new `parse-rejected` parser member or a schema change — rejected: an unreadable or
  refused `budget.json` reads as no reading with no line.
- `agent-run boot --statusline-echo` and a `statusline-source-unresolved` refusal — not built here (the cut,
  Implementation notes).
- A kill at any process a test did not start.
