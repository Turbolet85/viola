## Goal
The chunk makes the fake agent's hook traffic a contract against the recorded `viola verify` fixtures. For every
recorded `fixtures/claude/<ver>/` set, a root contract suite checks two things: the fake agent fires the spine
hooks in the recorded order, and it hands each hook exactly the bytes of that event's recorded fixture. A
deliberately introduced drift turns the suite red (per test-plan §6 Contract suite, §7 Fake agent; S8's
anti-drift intent). The suite witnesses one live drift before fixing it: the UserPromptSubmit payload drops the
fixture's trailing newline (research §Mechanism re-derivations).

Two folded items land here:
- **The receipt `size` resize oracle becomes change-driven, as test-plan §7 already states.** A key sent after a
  resize is the very race H2 measured, and the oracle no longer depends on one.
- **The hook classifier files the cross-session-message tag as harness origin.** Both the escaped form (the
  measured injected one) and the plain form count, when either starts the prompt. The founder ratified this
  Boundary widening live (P4 fork 1).

Two items move out, per the P4 operator forks with the overseer agreeing:
- matcher evaluation (CARRY 1), whose "recorded tool_name" premise is false at HEAD;
- S8 `annotations` forwarding, whose question-answer path does not exist yet.

Both go to "Dialog answers by dialog_id" (working-route :72). The fixture schema check already runs
(`tests/contract_fixture_hygiene.rs:46-59`) and stays as it is.

  `tool_name` exists, and the product registers no matcher (`plugin/hooks/hooks.json`). Implementing docs-only
  semantics would put unmeasured behaviour in the one component whose job is not to drift from the measured CLI.
  The P4 operator fork, with the overseer agreeing.
- Asserting S8 `annotations` forwarding here — the question-answer path (PreToolUse `updatedInput.answers` +
  `annotations`, test-plan :866/:1100) does not exist at HEAD, and arch :91 lands S8 with dialog answers.
- insta snapshots — rejected. The recorded fixture files are the pinned artifact, and a snapshot copy would
  duplicate them and have to move with every `--record` refresh. There is no new dependency; test-plan §6 is
  reworded at wrap.
- Trimming leading whitespace before the prefix match (the prototype's `trim_start`) — rejected. The raw start is
  the narrower class, and the `leading_space` case pins it.
- Filing only the plain, or only the escaped, cross-session form — rejected at P4 fork 1.
- Registering PreToolUse or PermissionRequest in the product `plugin/hooks/hooks.json` for a witness — banned.
  The dialog tier joins with its chunk (arch §Established Decisions [Deployment / Distribution]).
- Routing the contract's capture through `viola hook --capture` — banned. Only verify's probe plugin may register
  it (security-plan Decisions Log `2026-09-28`). The receipt's `stdin_hex` is the observation instead.
- A new `FAKE_AGENT_*` env seam, or any env read, for the size watcher or the suite — none is needed. A seam is a
  third boundary widening.
- Keeping the read-path `receipt_size` calls beside the watcher — rejected. Two mechanisms for one effect leave
  an unobservable, unkillable mutant (testing.md 2026-09-24).
- Raising `WITHIN` or any test bound to make a red pass — banned (testing.md 2026-09-28).
- Recording a new fixture set, or touching `fixtures/claude/`, in this chunk — out of scope. A live `--record` is
  operator-run and local.

## Implementation notes
- **Order:**
  - Steps 1 and 3 land first: the contract needs `stdin_hex`. Run the contract and record its red, then apply

  - Step 5's test lands and its red is recorded before step 4.
  - Step 6's table lands and its red is recorded before the `HARNESS_PREFIXES` edit.
  - Every red goes to `chunks/2026-09-29-fake-agent-drift-contract/evidence/`.
- **Hypothesis on byte equality:** the claim that re-serialising a `preserve_order` object reproduces the
  recorder's bytes (serde_json `=1.0.151` on both sides, `Cargo.toml:193`) is a HYPOTHESIS until step 1's first
  run. If the pre-fix red shows more than the trailing `0a` on UserPromptSubmit, stop and report the diff before
  fixing.
- **The watcher's lifetime:** it lives until the process exits, on `\x03` or EOF. It shares the `Receipt`
  (already `Mutex`-guarded) and owns its last size. With stdin/stdout not a terminal it writes nothing, so print
  mode and the piped paths are unaffected.
- **The `hook` receipt field on Windows:** `stdin_hex` of a 2.1.283 fixture is ≤ 1 KiB of hex (the four
  fixtures' sizes: `wc -c fixtures/claude/2.1.283/*.json` reads 290–438 B). The receipt is a test-home file under
  the secret scan, and the fixtures are scrubbed synthetic probe text.
- **Mutation:** the diff is a counted Rust delta: `src/bin` plus `crates/viola-agent-claude` with tests. It is
  judged at the Epoch 2b boundary `/andromeda-code-audit`, and every new branch needs a killing test:
  - the newline re-append (both cases);
  - the watcher's change comparison (the key-free witness);
  - the four prefixes, including a case whose leading operand is false (testing.md 2026-09-24 on `||` chains).
