The chunk also owns the mutants failure on `2834e4d` (run `35995290314`), folded on the overseer's direction and fixed at its cause. The root fixture chain's `Wrapper::wait_ready` never looks at the child it spawned, so a wrapper that exits without writing a role line spins to the 20 s readiness deadline. That deadline sits at cargo-mutants' 20 s floor, so both mutants were graded Timeout instead of caught (research.md §Measured facts). The fix has two parts, per the operator's P4 decision "fix by cause and by value":

- **By value:** every in-test deadline a root-package mutant can reach drops to ≤ 10 s, and so does the nextest mutants kill.
