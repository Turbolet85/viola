## Goal
This chunk turns test-plan §10 (Quality Gates & Coverage Targets) and the matching §9 CI rows into mechanical gates.

- **Harness verbs** (`viola-harness`): `run --coverage` (per-OS llvm-cov floors of lines 85 / functions 95 / regions 80), `run --fuzz-replay` (committed-corpus replay), and the `gate --require <suites>` verdict that closes every CI job and treats a missing artifact as a breach (test-plan §3 `run`, Internal harness subcommands `gate`; §10).
- **CI jobs**: a 1.96 MSRV job, a ubuntu fuzz-replay job with a `nightly.yml` time-box, and a coverage-instrumented per-OS `test` job.
- **Zero retries** becomes a checked property.
- **Fatal fmt/clippy** is already done (CARRY from `2026-09-24-observability-gates`) and is kept as it is.

The chunk also fixes the mutation gate's structural blind spot. cargo-mutants "does not yet understand conditional compilation … will report functions for other platforms as missed" (mutants.rs/limitations.html). That is why CI run `36005608858` on `39c3d2b` MISSED `secret_scan.rs:230:5`, the `#[cfg(not(unix))]` `file_mode` stub that Linux never compiles (research M1). The fix has three parts:
- `file_mode` becomes one body;
- the `mutants` job gains a `windows-2025` leg;
- a union verdict ("red only if no leg caught it") decides the gate.

This was operator-decided at P4: fix the gate now, not in Epoch 2.

The fuzz pipeline ships now with one genuine target, `ViolaName::try_new`, the validator every path join passes through (operator-decided at P4). test-plan §6's seven parsers each add their own target and corpus when they land.

The chunk closes two CARRYs. The zizmor pedantic `concurrency-limits` finding is declined with a recorded reason. The unscanned `mutants.out/` upload is removed.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Widening `--ignore-filename-regex`, lowering a floor, adding a nextest retry, excluding a mutant (`#[mutants::skip]`, `exclude_re`) or silencing a zizmor finding: rejected. The scope forbids weakening a gate, and security-plan says "never widen an ignore" (rules/security.md §Dependencies and CI).
- A `#[cfg(unix)]` test "observing the mode value" as the kill for `230:5`: rejected. That body is `#[cfg(not(unix))]` and never compiled on Linux (research M1). The existing unix tests already observe the mode.
- Keeping ubuntu-only mutation with a forward CARRY: rejected by the operator at P4 ("fix the gate now, not in Epoch 2").
- Per-leg strict mutation verdicts (both legs must be clean): rejected. Each leg misses the other OS's cfg bodies, so both would be red by construction. The union is the only verdict that holds.
- `cancel-in-progress` or any `concurrency:` group: rejected. A cancelled or replaced run drops that push's `--in-diff` mutation diff and its `always()` gate/upload chain.
- Scanning `mutants.out/` instead of removing the upload: rejected. The mutants job has no `target/e2e-home`, so `secret-scan` would be `empty-scope` red, and `outcomes.json`'s absolute argv paths are still obs-plan §8 Medium.
- A dtolnay toolchain action for MSRV or fuzz: rejected (architecture §CI/CD "no toolchain action"; security-plan's pinned-action set).
- A stub fuzz target for a parser that does not exist yet: rejected (scope item 4). Each later parser chunk adds its own.
- `fuzz/` as a root workspace member: rejected, because libfuzzer-sys pulls `cc`, which the deny C-build ban forbids (architecture §Module Boundaries).
- Uploading `target/lcov.info`: rejected, because it carries absolute paths (obs-plan §8 Medium).
