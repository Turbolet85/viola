## Constraints & rejected approaches
- A separate statusline run (Run E): rejected by the founder's fork 3 (`inputs#I1`). It would cost a start per round.
- A second `--settings` on Runs C or D: the probe document goes on Run B alone, so which of two flags wins stays
  unmeasured and unrelied (scope §3).
- Capturing the shell word as text: only the closed verdict is filed (the founder's fork-3 preview, `inputs#I1`).
- Routing the statusline capture through `HookEvent` or the `--answers` path: the arm is its own, beside them
  (security history 2026-09-28-capability-ledger-and-viola-verify; research.md §Measured facts 5).
- Running a shell anywhere in viola for the probe, or in the fake agent: `statusline_command` stays the only
  shell-out (security-plan §Security Anti-Patterns → Code Patterns).
- Writing or reading `~/.claude/settings.json`, `budget.json` or a snapshot in the probe: viola writes nothing under
  `~/.claude` (security-plan §Threat Model Summary).
- A hand-written `StatusLine` fixture, or the whole re-recorded set copied: the fake agent invents no shape before a
  recorded fixture (test-plan §7), and every other committed fixture keeps its bytes (the fixture guard).
- `--resume`, `/compact` and `claude agents --json` rows: moved off this entry by the founder's forks 1 and 2
  (`inputs#I1`).
- Any change to `send`, `typed_text`, `validate_paste_text`, the paste write, the budget gate or `hook statusline`'s
  pass-through: this chunk measures what they rely on and changes nothing about them.
- Raising a nextest kill line if a verify-driven case slows by the two fake-agent statusline firings: measure, then
  remove the slow work (testing.md 2026-09-28).
- A new fake-agent option, env seam or `config.json` key: the replay rides `--framing` (architecture §Cross-cutting
  Patterns, Config management).
- A second live round of either kind, a rehearsal start, or any other `claude` start: the cap is ten (`inputs#I1`). A
  red round is reported, never re-fired.
