## Goal
On a verified CLI the readiness gate stops refusing a `send` at the first quiet instant of a screen that holds no
compiled literal. It keeps waiting for the input box, and refuses `not-delivered` / `input-not-ready` only when its
bound has run out; the bound, `GATE_MAX_WAIT`, moves from 5 s to 8.5 s. A `send` issued while the CLI's paste hint
stands is then delivered once the input box returns. This is the founder's ruling of 2026-10-07T10:29Z (scope
§Authority), built in `viola_agent_claude::screen` as architecture §Established Decisions [Screen Model] places it.

The wait is on the automation side only. Research found that `send` reads the wheel once, before the gate's wait,
so a human who starts typing during a wait of seconds would be pasted over. After the wait `send` now reads the
wheel and the running turn again and refuses with nothing typed (the overseer's answer, inputs#I2; a11y-plan §1
and architecture §Established Decisions [Human Takeover / Wheel]).

The moved bound is what the stamped `quiet-period` row is checked against, so the chunk ends with one by-path
`viola verify` of `claude` 2.1.287: 5 live starts, fired once, never retried (inputs#I1). No ledger row, no
compiled literal, no fixture, no product string and no knob is added.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Reading the hint's own text (`paste again to expand`) — a new compiled literal is an eighteenth ledger row with
  a probe and a re-stamp, outside the one-round cap (architecture §Cross-cutting Patterns; the card's option 3).
- A config key, env var or flag for the bound, or an env seam to hold the hint open — banned, and a new seam is a
  boundary widening that inputs#I1 holds. The hold stays the fake agent's argv option.
- Editing `Gate::wait_ready`, `settled` or `box_wait` — the waiting fits the pure verdict through
  `waiting_since`; verify's functions read the constant by name.
- A separate constant keeping verify's fallback settle at 5 s — rejected by the overseer (inputs#I2): verify
  would stop waiting what the gate waits.
- An end-to-end case that blocks to the 8.5 s bound — rejected by the overseer (inputs#I2): the refusal at the
  bound is proved on the injected clock.
- Changing the `input-not-ready` hint line — left as it is (inputs#I2). A rewording is the founder's.
- Raising `WITHIN`, the `mutants` profile's own kill or any override other than the two `verify_window_` ones.
- Interrupting the gate's wait when the wheel moves — not built: the send reads the wheel after the wait, so the
  driver hears the refusal when the wait ends. `Gate` keeps knowing nothing of the wheel.
- `run --local-live` for the round — it drives PATH `claude`; the round names 2.1.287 by path.
- A second live round, a scratch session or a `--record` round — the cap is five starts (inputs#I1).
- Removing or moving the round's home, or anything under `target/e2e-home.disk/` — the home is left and named.
- Sleeping, retrying or skipping to sequence the keystroke case — it waits on the wrapper's `channel-request`
  line and on the `wheel` record.
