## Constraints & rejected approaches
- A raised timeout, an added retry, a nextest `retries` value, a serialised test group or a `#[ignore]` for the red —
  each hides a cause the founder's direction says to remove (testing.md 2026-09-28; zero-flake budget).
- A `--json` for `run` or `verify` — not owed (architecture [CLI Conventions]); `verify`'s stdout is its documented
  agent-readable outcome.
- `{"v":1,"refusal":…}` for revive's exit-1 outcomes — `refusal` is the channel `RefusalReason` set mapped to 10-14,
  and revive's refusals exit 1 (`inputs#I2` chose `error = the code`).
- Refusing on a malformed `VIOLA_DIR` — the variable is wrapper-to-child plumbing; a value that fails the shape check
  is no home, and the order falls through (hook's own reading of it is the same).
- Canonicalising a home that does not exist yet, or writing Windows' `\\?\` verbatim form into plugin-dir, cwd or
  settings arguments — the first fails where `run` and `list` must create or leave nothing; the second is a spelling
  the CLI was never measured on.
- Changing `revive`'s refused-check no-line shape (exemption 7) or any human pair's words — the founder has not ruled
  on the first, and several pairs are his.
- A `scanned` value taken from anything but the instance dir's own last component — no path, no pid (NEVER-log
  floor).
