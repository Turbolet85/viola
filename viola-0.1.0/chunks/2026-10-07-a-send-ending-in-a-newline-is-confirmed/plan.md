`not-delivered` / `no-prompt-submitted` after the 10 s window, with its own prompt filed `human` and the wheel
moved to the human. The cause is measured: the `claude` CLI drops a pasted text's last newline before
UserPromptSubmit, so the prompt is one byte short of the text the wrapper compares it with (scope items 8 and 10).

types it without its trailing newlines. Classification, the paste and the claim all read that one typed text, and

§Input Validation, Paste text row). The answer is the overseer's, PROVISIONAL for the founder, who is away: it is
