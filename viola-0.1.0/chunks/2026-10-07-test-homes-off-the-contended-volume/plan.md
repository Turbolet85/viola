## Goal
On the Linux dev host, `<workspace>/target/e2e-home` becomes a link to a per-user, owner-only directory on tmpfs,
so the 52 MB pinned copy every test home takes no longer waits on the shared volume's dirty-page budget while
another builder links (per test-plan §5 Setup / teardown lifecycle and architecture §Occupied Resources →
Repository; the mechanism per inputs#I3). The homes' path string does not change, no product crate changes, and
no CI runner has a link, so CI keeps writing its homes to a real disk through the real `sync_all()`.

A small test-side keeper, in the two places that create homes, re-creates the link's target after a reboot clears
it and refuses a target that is not an owner-only real directory. G2's walk is written so that a linked scope is
descended.

The harness then creates and deletes outside the working directory through the link. That is a boundary
widening, ratified by the founder's own live answer of 2026-10-07T07:25Z, relayed by the overseer (inputs#I6).

The chunk's claim is a hypothesis until its last step: that the pinned copy is what stalls a real start. The
proof is one contended reading inside another builder's natural link: the stall measured on the shared volume
and, in the same seconds, the verify-driven tests green on the new backing.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- A mount the founder runs at the desk — priced and not chosen at P4: root at the desk before the green reading
  and after every reboot, a missing mount falls back to the shared volume with no signal. It stays the fallback
  if the suite cannot pass over a link, and taking it is a revision of this plan.
- A link with no keeper — not chosen at P4: after every reboot every start fails until the target is re-made by
  hand.
- A backing chosen by an environment variable — rejected: `pre-push`'s children run under `env -i` with `HOME` and
  `PATH` only, so the value would not reach them, and any assignment that carries it is a second widening.
- Removing or conditioning `sync_all()` — rejected: it is product durability, and inputs#I3 read the stall with no
  fsync at all.
- Raising a nextest kill, `WITHIN` or any wait, adding a retry, parking a test — rejected: a timing red is closed
  by its cause.
- A synthetic writer on the shared volume — not without the overseer's word after the cap (inputs#I6).
- A committed test that times a contended start — rejected: the reading is evidence, never a suite member.
- The keeper removes nothing, creates no `home`, creates no parent of the target, and follows no target that is
  itself a link.
- `--probe` in `scripts/g2-zero-panics.sh` is not edited: what CI runs before G2 stays as it is.
- No `.andromeda/` master is edited by /implement.
- A red on the backing is never worked around: steps 6 and 8 name the stop.
