The MAX_FRAME + 1 send: the server reads to the bound, answers `-32600` and closes (the designed
refusal); on macOS the client was still writing the frame's tail into a smaller socket buffer and got

sends accept EPIPE / reset on the WRITE only, then still require the `-32600` reply and the closed
connection.
