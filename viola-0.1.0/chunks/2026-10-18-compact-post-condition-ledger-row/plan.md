`session-start` of cause `compact` carrying the SAME `agent_session_id` the send slot remembered — instead of reading
`unconfirmable` at once as it has since chunk 2026-10-14-resume-and-compact-ledger-rows (architecture §Established
Decisions [Delivery Confirmation]; research.md §Measured facts 1). The confirmation waits a compiled window of its own,
`COMPACT_CONFIRM_WINDOW` = 40 s, PROVISIONAL, beside the unchanged 10 s `CONFIRM_WINDOW_FALLBACK`: one compaction was
measured at 18.8 s, and the send's total (the 8.5 s gate wait + the paste + the window) must stay under the MCP client's
tool-call timeout, known only to exceed 50 s on 2.1.287 (research.md §Measured facts 2). An unverified CLI still reads
`unconfirmable` at once, with no window.

## Constraints & rejected approaches
- Widening `CONFIRM_WINDOW_FALLBACK` for every send — architecture [Delivery Confirmation] keeps 10 s for the rest, and
  the bound would cover sends that need none.
- A window above 41.5 s, or a separate MCP-only window — the total would pass the measured MCP ceiling, or the two
  surfaces would read one send differently (layout-templates: a tool result is the verb's `--json` document less `v`).
- Confirming on a bare `compact` cause, or on a screen reading — the same-id check is the measured shape, and screen
  content is never evidence (obs-plan §11; a11y-plan §11 Universal).
- A hand-written `compact-1` fixture, or a fake-agent payload built without the recorded variant — test-plan §7; tests
  history 2026-10-10-viola-revive.
- Copying the whole re-recorded 2.1.287 set — every other committed fixture keeps its bytes (the fixture guard).
- A third live round, a rehearsal session, a `run --local-live` firing, a plugin start, or any `claude` start beyond
  the round and its one re-run — the founder's cap (`inputs#I3`).
- A seventh start for the probe — the founder chose the sixth Run B paste (`inputs#I3`, fork 1).
- Any of CARRY 2's readings or a trust-dialog keystroke or `~/.claude.json` write — moved to "Plugin precedence live
  readings" (`inputs#I3`, forks 3 and 4).
- A cross-process human-key case held through the 40 s window — no test may wait out a product deadline end to end
  (test-plan §11 Universal); the confirm wait sits on the slot's condvar under the flight lock (`slot.rs:141`), which
  the pump's human copy never takes, and `send_under_the_paste_hint_a_human_key_during_the_gate_wait_wins` covers the
  human path; a11y-plan §8 gains the bound line at the wrap.
- A new `detail`, `event` or `subject` value — the expiry reuses `no-prompt-submitted` (obs-plan §6).
- A new fake-agent option or seam — the compact stem reaches the fake agent through `framing_stem`.
- Raising a nextest kill line if a verify-driven case slows by the sixth paste — measure, then remove the slow work
  (testing.md 2026-09-28).
