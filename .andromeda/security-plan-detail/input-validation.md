- **Boundary:** Paste text: channel `send.text`, MCP `send.text`, CLI `send` stdin/`--file`
  - **What to validate:** **Rule (exact set):** multi-line text is normal (relays, plans and free-text answers are multi-line, brief §4.1 S1), so three controls are **allowed**: LF (U+000A), CR (U+000D) and TAB (U+0009). Everything else in this set is **refused**: every other C0 control (U+0000–U+0008, U+000B–U+000C, U+000E–U+001F, including ESC U+001B above all), DEL (U+007F) and every C1 control (U+0080–U+009F, including CSI U+009B). The check runs over decoded `char`s of the UTF-8 `&str`, never over raw bytes, because UTF-8 continuation bytes 0x80–0xBF are not C1 controls. This stops a bracketed-paste breakout through an embedded `ESC[201~`. **Reject, don't strip:** a text that holds a refused character is refused whole; no refused character is ever removed and the rest typed. **The typed text, an inner CR as one LF and the trailing CR and LF removed, allowed characters only:** after `validate_paste_text` has passed on the text as received, the wrapper's `send` takes the typed text once (`viola_agent_claude::hook::typed_text`: every CR LF pair as one LF, every other CR as one LF, and every trailing CR and LF removed, in any order; it holds no CR, and nothing else changes, not a TAB, a space, or an LF that is not at the very end), and from there on it is the only text `send` uses: the local-command classification (a listed local command followed by LF, CR or CRLF is that command: `/clear` and a CRLF is `/clear`), the in-flight text the delivery-confirmation exact match compares, `text_bytes` and the paste. A text that ends in CR, LF or CRLF and holds a refused character is still refused `control-character` with nothing typed.
 The LF removal is the founder's ruling, live, 2026-10-07T15:21Z, every option and the `/clear`-plus-newline consequence shown to him, relayed by the overseer (chunk 2026-10-07-a-send-ending-in-a-newline-is-confirmed); the CR and CRLF removal is the founder's ruling, live, 2026-10-09, the options shown, relayed by the overseer (chunk 2026-10-09-epoch-3-cleanup); typing a CR or a CR LF inside a text as the LF the CLI submits is the founder's ruling, live in the overseer's dialog, 2026-10-09T16:51Z, the options shown, relayed by the operator (chunk 2026-10-09-inner-cr-and-crlf-in-a-sent-text). Only CR and LF, both allowed characters, are rewritten or removed: the set of accepted texts is unchanged, the typed text of a valid text is valid and holds no character that is neither in the received text nor LF (a 512-case property pins it), no refused character is typed or removed, a raw channel client gets the same rule from the wrapper, and the delivery-confirmation match stays an exact equality with no tolerance on the submitted side. **An empty typed text is refused:** a text whose typed text is empty, the empty text included, is refused `not-delivered` / `empty-text` (`NotDelivered::EmptyText`, exit 13) on both sides: by the client (`refusal_of` in `src/cmd/send.rs`, which reads a refused character first and then the empty typed text) before any frame, with a `send-refused{side:"client"}` line, and by the wrapper's `send` directly after the typed text is taken and before the first wheel read, with nothing reserved, issued or typed (the name, the condition and the hint are the founder's ruling of 2026-10-09, relayed; the rung is the overseer's).
 **Where it runs:** `viola_core::validate_paste_text(&str) -> Result<(), NotDelivered>` (`Err(NotDelivered::ControlCharacter)`), called client-side before sending (CLI `viola send` in `src/cmd/send.rs`: exit 13 before any frame and a `send-refused{side:"client"}` line; the MCP `send` tool in `viola-mcp`, since chunk 2026-10-17-mcp-server-for-drivers: `validate_paste_text`, then the empty-typed-text check, each refused as `{"refusal":"not-delivered","detail":"control-character"|"empty-text"}` with one client-side `send-refused` line, before any frame) and again by the wrapper's `send` (`src/run/send.rs`) before anything is typed — first in its refusal order, and authoritative. The paste itself is one `ESC[200~…ESC[201~` + CR `write_all` of the typed text through `viola-pty`'s `PasteHandle`. **Wire outcome:** refusal `not-delivered` with detail `control-character`, then `not-delivered` with detail `empty-text`, both checked before the Conventions refusal order.
  - **How (stack-specific):** `viola-core`; `src/cmd/send.rs`; `src/run/send.rs`; `viola-pty` `PasteHandle`
