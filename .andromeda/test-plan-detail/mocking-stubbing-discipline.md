## 8. Mocking & Stubbing Discipline

<!-- detail: values · mocking -->

- Time: sync crates use mock_instant 0.6.1 (global mode) or the injected `viola_core::Clock` for heartbeat 1 s / 5 s and the send confirmation window. `budget_override_until` is no clock-seam case: the budget gate's decision takes `chrono::DateTime<Utc>` as an argument, and its expiry is a pure-function unit case (§4, chunk 2026-10-10-budget-governor). Async crates (`viola-mcp`) use `#[tokio::test(start_paused = true)]` from tokio 1.53.3 `test-util` for any timer the MCP adapter itself owns. The `wait` `timeout_ms` deadline runs in the wrapper process, and only the injected `viola_core::Clock` in the §4 root-bin case drives it. A paused tokio clock in a test never reaches a spawned wrapper (§11 Universal). `chrono::Utc::now()` is read only through the clock seam, with two exceptions as landed: the budget gate's three evaluation points read it directly and hand the instant to the pure decision (as read in `src/run/send.rs` and `src/cmd/run.rs` at the chunk 2026-10-10-budget-governor wrap; that read named the gate's call sites only and was no census of `Utc::now()`); and `LinkSet::line` stamps the link line's `since` with it directly (`src/run/links.rs:92`), so root cases assert `since` by shape only, through `is_clock_time` in `tests/cli_links.rs`, itself held by `is_clock_time_reads_the_shape_only`.
