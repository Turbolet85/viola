## Analysis

**1. Downstream readiness.** No patch needed.
- `scrub-leak` and `axe-rule-not-run` are in the closed list with `serious` severity, and each id has one meaning.
- Strip positions are valid HTML. The lint paths and configs (`crates/viola-ui/`, `e2e-web/eslint.config.js`, `.htmlvalidate.json` including the `tbody` extension) are named.
- Aggregation is `if: always()`, the applicable-SC rule is defined per "Applies in v1" value, and `violation_type_counts` keeps `scrub-leak` apart from `csp-console`.

**2. Scope faithfulness.** Two gaps.
- The tui boundary clauses are fine: all three have their own nextest cases (§3 Keyboard tooling, P4).
- **Gap 1: the CLI C0/C1 escaping is only restated.** P6 says "C0/C1 escaped except `\n` / `\t`" but gives no input, verbs or check. Upstream (security, line 78) scopes it to human-mode `wait`/`last` and to `list` rows from `claude agents --json`. As written, `setup-project` cannot build this check.
- **Gap 2: some ATIS states have no check.** `TAPE connecting` and `TAPE live` are in the Section 1 entity and the §4 ATIS catalog row, which claims SC 1.4.1. They get no check anywhere: the §3 SC 1.4.1 state-word list omits them and the `skipped N · N · N` counters. A grep for `TAPE connecting` found it only in the entity, the catalog and the §6 token list.
- Pointers resolve, and the not-assertable entities (v1.x brakes and the rest) stay out of `sc-coverage.json`.

**3. Tool anchoring.** Versions agree across the three places they appear. The option-ordering claim is labelled as an assumption and has a machine check. `jq` is stated as catalog-less transport. The §3 per-SC map, however, credits three tools to SCs that research assigns elsewhere:
- **SC 2.3.1 row:** credits lint `no-distracting-elements`. Research (eslint-plugin-lit-a11y) assigns that rule to SC 2.2.2. Research says no axe rule covers 2.3.1, so its evidence should be the computed-style walk plus the `reducedMotion` override (the Playwright block says "SC 2.2.2 / 2.3.1 design contract").
- **SC 2.5.1/2.5.2/2.5.4/2.5.7 row:** credits `click-events-have-key-events`. Research assigns that rule to SC 2.1.1, together with `tabindex-no-positive` and `no-autofocus`.
- **SC 1.3.1 row:** credits html-validate `prefer-native-element`. Research assigns it to SC 4.1.2. Research's 1.3.1 rules include `no-missing-references`, which the draft never names even though the tape's `aria-labelledby` depends on it, and `no-multiple-main`.

**4. Tier calibration.** No patch needed. The §10 state list matches the P1–P5 axe verdicts and the §5 layouts (scrolled-up, skip link focused, expanded, `human`, stale, 404/405, boxed ATIS states, `unconfirmable`). Budgets are tied to `duration_ms` / `stats.duration` and checked by Aggregation.

**5. WCAG mapping.** One issue.
- SC 2.4.11 triggers agree between §3 and §5, the SC 2.5.8 spacing exception is defined, and the A/AA levels are present.
- **Issue:** §10 still says a generic "**Zero WCAG AA violations**". That can read as a WCAG 2.2 AA claim, which the §11 Strategy ban and D-A11Y-04 forbid, and it names no SC set.

**6. Design-token binding.** No patch needed. The skip link and anchor are bound to `--ink`/`--surface-bay`, and `--focus-ring`/`--surface-strip` is scoped to a ratio check only. The text-spacing selector list is defined, no raw values appear, and forced-colors wording matches the design.

**7. Anti-patterns.** No patch within budget. § CI and § Universal now name the `@axe-core/playwright` pick. The § Strategy "NEVER skip POUR" ban is still template wording; it is deferred because the patch budget went to higher dimensions.
