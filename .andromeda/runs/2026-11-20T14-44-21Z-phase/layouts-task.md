A viola run, and the start of viola revive where it shares the version gate, that meets a claude CLI version with no viola verify stamp gets that version stamped without a human running viola verify by hand, then goes on verified.
The driver's sends and answers are no longer refused unverified-cli for a version so stamped.
The form of the automatic verify (before the session starts, beside it, a separate verb or flag, a config key turning it on or off) is the founder's at this chunk's P3; P4 plans only the form he answers.
A red verify of a new version does not loop: the product says which rows failed, records the failed attempt so it is not repeated, names what clears it, and leaves the session to the human as today.
What the product asks or tells the user before the first automatic verify, and what a user who never wants it does, is also the founder's at P3.
