Focusing a named live session brings its window forward on its workspace on Hyprland, for a session viola opened and for one started by hand, read live at R3 as `r3: green hand=focused opened=focused` in the chunk's evidence/r3.md, the active window after each focus being that session's terminal window. With no window found it refuses `window-not-found` (exit 1, no dispatch). With no `hyprctl` reachable it refuses `unsupported` (exit 1, nothing spawned); a host with `hyprctl` and no compositor answering refuses `unreachable`. The two refusals are proven over the fakes by `focus_with_no_window_of_its_session_is_window_not_found` and `focus_off_hyprland_is_unsupported`.
