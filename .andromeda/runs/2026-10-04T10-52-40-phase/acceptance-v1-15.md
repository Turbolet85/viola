In `tests/cli_answer.rs`, on all three CI OSes: a plan dialog raised by PreToolUse(`ExitPlanMode`) and one raised by PermissionRequest(`ExitPlanMode`) are each logged exactly once as kind `plan` and are answered by `viola answer` as `{behavior: approve|revise, message?}`. An approve emits PreToolUse `allow` (insta-pinned), and an approve on a PermissionRequest-raised plan emits no body, so approval never depends on the ignored permission-request path. A revise emits PermissionRequest `deny` + `message` (insta-pinned) with no second plan event. The classification and both decision bodies come from the one S7 ledger row (`plan-approve-revise`), whose post-condition passes in the 2.1.287 stamp.
