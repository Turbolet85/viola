@task_prose part=chunk
Under viola run, a prompt submitted by a CLI-scheduled wake is told apart from a prompt the human typed.
A wake that fires leaves the wheel with whoever held it: the driver's next send is confirmed, not refused human-typing, and the next question dialog is held for the driver.
A prompt the human types still takes the wheel. When the wrapper cannot tell the two apart, it takes the conservative side and files the prompt as human.
This phase finds the means (the CLI sends no source field) and names each candidate, its false positive and what an older or unverified CLI gets.
It carries the open third reading of the placeholder hint's cell attribute, the open intermittent rig rehearsal red and the boot-timeout watch. No live start is made and no figure is added to the documentation.
@task_item n=1 part=needs
The wheel's holder after a submitted prompt: a prompt told as a fired scheduled wake leaves the wheel where it was, while a prompt the human typed still takes it (capability v2-36).
@task_item n=2 part=needs
The driver's send path after a fired wake: viola send is confirmed and its output reads as a confirmed send, not the human-typing refusal line with its hint. This holds in the human form and in the --json document.
@task_item n=3 part=needs
The human-typing refusal itself stays for a prompt the human really typed, with its refusal word, its hint and its exit code unchanged. The wrapper's own repeat of the send refusal stays the same as well.
@task_item n=4 part=needs
The question dialog after a fired wake is held for the driver. viola wait reports it to the driver as a dialog and viola answer can answer it. The dialog is not routed to the human.
@task_item n=5 part=needs
The wheel state that viola list shows (the WHEEL column of the board and the wheel field of the --json document) reflects the holder kept through a fired wake. It does not flip to human.
@task_item n=6 part=needs
The origin of a submitted prompt (human or wake) is decided from the UserPromptSubmit hook payload and from the human's keys read by the wrapper's pty pump. The payload carries no source key on the CLI versions read so far (2.1.325, 2.1.326).
@task_item n=7 part=needs
The conservative fallback: when the means cannot tell a wake from a typed prompt, or the CLI is older or unverified, the prompt is filed origin human and the wheel moves to the human. No output may present a guess as a wake.
@task_item n=8 part=needs
The turn end's scheduled_wakes count, together with the normalised event for a submitted prompt, is an input the means may rest on.
@task_item n=9 part=needs
