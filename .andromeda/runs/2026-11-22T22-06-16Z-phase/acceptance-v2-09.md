A session's declared parent and role (`viola run --parent`/`--role`, else the inherited `VIOLA_NAME` as the parent) are recorded at its launch as one `lineage` event `{parent, role}` in its own `events.ndjson`, right after the start's `wheel` line, and in its snapshot, and read back the same by `viola list --json` after a wrapper kill and a cut snapshot (`e5_the_replay_reads_the_lineage_the_wrapper_wrote`); the board shows the fleet as trees — each child row under its parent's, its NAME indented two spaces per depth, the same characters piped and on a terminal (`list_draws_a_declared_child_under_its_parent_piped`, the `board_tree_*` cases); a reused name started with no parent has none (`list_a_reused_name_started_without_a_parent_has_none`); a revived session keeps its parent and role (`revive_keeps_the_recorded_parent_and_role`); no parent edge changes the wheel, the links or whether a send is accepted (`lineage_changes_no_wheel_link_or_send`, `lineage_is_read_by_no_wheel_link_send_or_answer_path`).
