## Bindings
- **Error recovery ↔ Plain language**: each new closed code carries both the hint-last form (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19) and the rule that it never stands without the word `unable` (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:270).
- **Error recovery ↔ the macOS carve-out**: the server-verify refusal is held on windows-2025 and ubuntu-latest only (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:261), so the refusal's form (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19) carries no macOS case that reads the match as a comparison, while the 0755 directory refusal is a macOS case of its own.
- **Error recovery ↔ the TUI boundary**: the refusal form addresses a client's own stderr (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19); a wrapper refusing a connecting client on euid writes no such line to its outer terminal, its diagnostic going to `diagnostics/` (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:55).
- **Error recovery ↔ the keystroke path**: a directory or peer refusal on the wrapper's connection path (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19) never blocks, refuses or delays a human key (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:334).
- **Error recovery ↔ output discipline**: the new `unable` lines and their details (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19) stay uncoloured (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:340) and escape C0/C1 except `\n` and `\t` (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:53).
- **Strict-modes refusals ↔ the connect path's order**: the strict-modes reading on client verbs (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:37) stays first ahead of the no-endpoint reading (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:79), and the plan states where the directory and euid checks stand between them and the first frame.
