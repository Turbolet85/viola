- **2026-09-25-pty-wrapper-on-windows** — §3 Keyboard test harness Tooling and §6 CLI equivalent: on Windows ConPTY emits its own `ESC[?9001h ESC[?1004h ESC[?25l ESC[2J ESC[m ESC[H`, an OSC 0 title and `ESC[?25h` on every spawn. This is a direct trap for the extract's outer-PTY `waiting:` assertion ("no `\r`, cursor-movement or erase sequence"). The `wait` check must be phrased as absence of viola-emitted redraw/erase/SGR (or an exactly-once `waiting: <name>` literal), or be OS-scoped, or it fails on Windows. The same applies to the "no SGR" checks if any runs under an outer PTY.
