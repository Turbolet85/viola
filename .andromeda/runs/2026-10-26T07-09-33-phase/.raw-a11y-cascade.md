## Bindings
- **scope-entities (CLI verbs) ↔ cognitive-errors**: the six verbs' refusal on a failed verification is CLI-verb output in human and `--json` modes (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/a11y-scope-summary.md:8), and its shape is the `unable … hint:` pattern with a hint-free `--json` document (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19); an unreadable or newer `snapshot.json` under the held fork keeps that same refusal unchanged.
- **scope-entities (tui boundary) ↔ cognitive-timeouts**: the hook's fail-open writes zero terminal bytes and delays no keystroke (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/a11y-scope-summary.md:20), and every bound the verification holds falls on the driver or the hook, never the human (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:251).
- **path-p6-board ↔ cognitive-errors**: a refused `wait` / `last` keeps linear static output (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/aria-patterns-roles.md:27) and gives its reason in text as the `unable … hint:` pair (.andromeda/a11y-plan-detail/cognitive-accessibility.md:19).
- **scope-entities (plugin / MCP) ↔ security-plan**: the hook and MCP surfaces are not-assertable (.andromeda/a11y-plan.md:40), so the MCP `instance-unreachable` code and the hook's exit 0 with empty streams are held by the security plan's error rules, not by an a11y assertion.
