## Relevance
partial — no UI surface is built. The chunk touches a11y in three places: the tui boundary cases (scope item 3), the epoch-boundary mutation audit that a11y's output-discipline invariants lean on (items 1–2), and the pre-push `run --browser` re-judgement (item 4).

## Constraints
- The CLI/TUI output-discipline invariants (P4, P6) must pass on every OS leg. Where they live in crate tests, they rely on the epoch-boundary mutation audit (`run --mutants`) for coverage (per a11y-plan §10 Standard+ invariants). The surviving mutants this chunk disposes include the cfg(unix) outer-PTY code (viola-pty `HostTerminal::enter` ×4 and `host_size`). These fall under that coverage, so each one must be killed by a test or recorded as equivalent with its argument. Research should establish which of the 12 cfg(unix) mutants and which viola-e2e mutants sit under P4/P6 assertions.
- P4 on the tui surface requires three portable-pty outer-PTY nextest cases on all three OS legs, one per boundary clause: (1) zero viola-originated bytes; (2) human keystrokes delivered unblocked past the current atomic paste; (3) focus, mouse and resize sequences do not move the wheel (per a11y-plan §4 P4; §3 Keyboard test harness → Tooling). On Linux, clause (1) is a byte-for-byte comparison against an unwrapped fake-agent run under the same outer PTY, not a literal-absence check. Two questions are for research to answer by measuring on the host: whether the in-repo TUI set measured under item 3 includes all three cases, and whether they pass byte-for-byte there.
- The ConPTY variants of the zero-bytes clause (inbox and sideload backends) check that viola's literals are absent. They are witnessed only on the `windows-2025` leg (per a11y-plan §3 Keyboard test harness → Tooling). A Linux-host 5/0 or any other host result must not be counted as covering them.
- The browser suite runs on all three CI legs, but the a11y verdict is judged on the ubuntu leg only. `browser-missing` (a failed `npm ci` or a missing Chromium) is a failure, never a skip (per a11y-plan §9 Pipeline integration; §3 CI integration). If the migrated native Linux pre-push stage carries `run --browser`, it carries the same rule. Retiring the `windows-tests` `run --browser` mirror removes no a11y verdict leg.
- The cli and tui surfaces are boundary/output-discipline only, with no WCAG conformance claim (per a11y-plan §1 Scope Summary, tui row; §11 Strategy). Mutation-scoring records and pre-push stage outputs must not describe the tui cases as a11y conformance evidence.
- Zero flakiness: `retries: 0` and no `test.skip` / `#[ignore]` parking, mirroring the zero-violation invariant (per a11y-plan §10 Cross-input parallel). This applies to the 32-of-246 harness failures and the 13 C3 reds: they are fixed at the cause, never parked.

## Patterns to follow
- The outer-PTY boundary-case shape: `viola-fake-agent` as the child, a driver `send`, a refused automation send (`human-typing`), then exit. On Linux/macOS, compare against an unwrapped run (per a11y-plan §3 Keyboard test harness → Tooling). New tests that kill viola-pty survivors should follow this shape.
- The browser leg goes only through the 5-command discipline: `scripts/agent-run.sh run --browser` → the locked Playwright CLI in `e2e-web/` → `gate --require …playwright` (per a11y-plan §3 CI integration → Command; §9 Pipeline integration).
- Mutant survivors are disposed the way M1's were: killed by a new or strengthened test, or recorded as equivalent with an argument. The not-measurable record for the 13th mutant takes the same form (scope item 2). For output-discipline code, the a11y-plan §10 coverage clause is what makes this binding.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- NEVER block, refuse or delay a human keystroke in `viola run` past the current atomic paste. NEVER count focus, mouse or resize sequences as editing (per a11y-plan §11 Keyboard). A mutant-killing test or harness change must not weaken the P4 clause (2)/(3) assertions.
- NEVER treat `browser-missing` as a skip, and never judge the a11y verdict on the Windows or macOS legs (per a11y-plan §11 CI). NEVER park a spec or test with skip, fixme or a quarantine tag (per a11y-plan §11 SLO).
- NEVER parse the rendered `claude` TUI screen for content, and never take screenshots as a11y evidence (per a11y-plan §11 Universal, R7). Boundary checks compare viola-originated bytes only.

## Contract bindings
- a11y ↔ tests: the epoch-boundary mutation audit (`run --mutants`, test-plan §10) covers the P4/P6 output-discipline invariants that live in crate tests (per a11y-plan §10 Standard+ invariants). This chunk's boundary-tier scoring of viola-e2e and the cfg(unix) set is where that binding is exercised.
- a11y ↔ tests harness: the pre-push native stage's `run --browser` binds to a11y-plan §3 CI integration and §9 Pipeline integration (`gate --require …playwright`, `browser-missing` as failure).
- a11y ↔ security: to reach the browser suite, the native stage needs the pinned Node and the locked Playwright's Chromium on the `env -i` PATH. Whether any PATH entry beyond the security-plan's admitted `<home>/.local/viola-node/bin` widens the boundary is the founder's call at P4 (scope item 4). This extract makes no ruling on it.
