Every push produces a CI run on `ubuntu-latest` whose seven `ci.yml` jobs (test, perf, lint, msrv, fuzz-replay, release, supply-chain) read `verdict: green` on this chunk's pushed sha through `ci.py conclusion`: build, fmt, fatal lint, the tokio-free check of the sync crates, the dependency policy (`cargo deny check` with its advisories family green and no ignore), zizmor and tests; the perf gates run in their own `perf` job; each job checks its gate tools present first; `AGENT_RUN_KEEP_HOMES=1` keeps test homes until G2, G4 and the secret scan have read them; every test-home upload waits on the secret scan; and the last scheduled `nightly.yml` advisory run (no code change) is recorded with its conclusion in the chunk's `evidence/ci.md`.
