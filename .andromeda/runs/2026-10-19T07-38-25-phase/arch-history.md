- **2026-10-04-dialog-answers-by-dialog-id** — (the dialog tier served: hook.dialog, answer, the deadline, nine hooks) set the values this chunk moves. [Hook Contract]'s dialog deadline went from an open item to `viola_core::DIALOG_DEADLINE` = 60 s PROVISIONAL. The dialog hooks' `hooks.json` `timeout` became 75, above the deadline and test-pinned. The hook's reply read was bounded at `DIALOG_DEADLINE + 5 s`, with one stdout write. `hooks.json` grew to nine entries (PreToolUse `AskUserQuestion|ExitPlanMode` and PermissionRequest at `timeout` 75). It also landed `wait` returning a pending dialog first through `WaitFeed::appending_dialog` / `dialog_settled`, `answer`'s refusal order, and `run/dialog.rs`'s `DialogSlot`. Why it matters: the 60 s value was "the overseer's founder-delegated call", never the founder's own word. A longer or configurable hold moves all three related values (deadline, rendered `timeout`, reply bound) together, and the hold length may be a founder fork.
- **2026-10-04-wait-and-last** — fixed `wait`'s wake rule. It wakes on `EventKind::WAIT_WAKE` through the `WaitFeed` (one Mutex + Condvar, the hook line appended inside the lock), and only hook lines signal it. Why it matters: a wrapper-appended expired-hold record would not wake `wait` under this rule. Making it wake changes this landed contract, so the change must be stated, or the record must be filed as a hook-sourced line.
