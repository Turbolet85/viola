The only other variables any `viola` build reads are the two test seams `FAKE_AGENT_PUMP_DELAY_MS` (capped at 5 s) and `FAKE_AGENT_HOOK_PANIC` (exact value `1`, a forced fail-open `hook` panic), both compiled only under `cfg(feature = "fake-agent")` and absent from release builds, and, in every build, `NO_COLOR`, `TERM` and `COLORTERM`, read by viola's own colour layer (`src/human/style.rs`, `viola list`'s human mode only) to decide whether and how deep its SGR bytes are written (the founder's own word, chunk 2026-10-17-cli-output-discipline): they configure nothing, disable no control and widen no redaction. viola's reader is the only one because clap is built without its `color` feature: that feature's anstream → anstyle-query route read `CLICOLOR`, `CLICOLOR_FORCE`, `NO_COLOR`, `TERM`, `COLORTERM` and `CI` and was closed at chunk 2026-09-28-cli-output-tokens (as measured at its research F1: `CLICOLOR_FORCE=1 viola --help` into a pipe gave 25 ESC bytes before, 0 after); a dependency or feature that reads an environment variable breaks this rule like a direct read does.
