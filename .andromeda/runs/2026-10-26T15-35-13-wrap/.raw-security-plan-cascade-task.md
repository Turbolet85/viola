## New facts
- F1 · authentication-authorization · `viola link` / `viola unlink` call `viola_state::strict::check_instance(<absolute home>, <home>/instances/<driven>)` before `client::start`, so before any role file, log or snapshot read. Then come the liveness-only pre-check and the server identity check through `cmd::client::connect`. They take no dated strict-modes gap (the founder's word at P4, `inputs#I3`).
- F2 · authentication-authorization · The verbs' strict-modes refusal is exit 21 (as for `list` / `plugin install`, not `revive`'s exit 1). The human form is the fixed instance-state `unable` / `hint:` pair. `--json` prints exactly `{"v":1,"error":"instance-unreachable","detail":null}` with stderr empty. No line goes to any log, and the directory's mode is left as found.
- F3 · authentication-authorization · `tests/cli_links.rs` `link_verbs_refuse_a_widened_instance_before_any_log` (Unix) proves that a group-writable driven instance is refused before any log, with its mode as found. Its remove-the-guard control goes red when the check is moved after `client::start`.
- F4 · threat-model-summary · The driven instance's wrapper serves the channel methods `link` and `unlink` (nine served methods with `hook.event`). `link` records the pair and answers `{"ok":{"since":<the link line's ts>,"already_linked":<bool>}}`; a held pair appends nothing. `unlink` removes the pair and answers `{"ok":{}}` whether or not a pair existed. Neither opens a span or writes a process-log line.
- F5 · input-validation · The wrapper's `link` / `unlink` read `driver` and `driven` as strings through `ViolaName::try_new`. It refuses as `-32602` (`data: null`, no line written) a missing, non-string or invalid name, a `driven` that is not the serving wrapper's own name, and `driver` equal to `driven`. It refuses the self-pair only in the wrapper, never client-side. Neither method reads `from`.
- F6 · input-validation · `tests/channel_links.rs` `channel_link_names_are_validated_by_the_wrapper` sends raw frames past every CLI check, through `viola_channel::Client::request`. It proves the bad-name, foreign-driven and self-pair `-32602` refusals with no line written.
- F7 · threat-model-summary · New CLI verbs `viola link <driver> <driven> [--json]` and `viola unlink <driver> <driven> [--json]` act on the driven instance's endpoint. Their names come from `ViolaName` parsing, and their `from` comes from `VIOLA_NAME` when it is set. They are not on the MCP tool list.
- F8 · error-handling · For the verbs, a wrapper JSON-RPC error (`-32602` included) is exit 20 through `client::fault`. `Dead` / `ServerUnverified` is exit 21. A `Refused` reply or an `ok` of an unexpected shape is an internal error, exit 1. A `record` / `remove` failure in the wrapper is `-32603`.
- F9 · data-protection · A product path now writes the `unlink` event line to `events.ndjson` (`source: wrapper`, `data: {driver, driven}`, names only). The wrapper's process log holds no driver name (asserted by `link_verbs_log_the_channel_pair_and_no_names`).

- F11 · security-anti-patterns · The served `link` / `unlink` methods are mapped to the IPC controls (the owner-only endpoint, the client server check, `check_instance` before the frame, `ViolaName` params checked in the wrapper) by the founder's word `inputs#I3`.
