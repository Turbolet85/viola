## Relevant amendment history
- **2026-09-28-capability-ledger-and-viola-verify** — (verify's outcome on stdout, the uninstrumented `hook --capture` arm) verify's agent-readable outcome became one stdout step line per row plus the last `stamped <version>  N pass  N fail` line, with refusals as the stderr `unable:` / `hint:` pair. The `hook --capture` arm is uninstrumented by design (no `VIOLA_*`, no obs init, no role or detail line; §10 bounded exemption 5). The same entry kept §6's rule that every child spawn logs a `process-start` / `process-exit` pair. Run B's framing waits count that arm's captures, so a restated wait must not add instrumentation to the arm. Verify's stdout lines are owned output, and a test seam must not write into them.
- **2026-09-29-verify-stamped-test-homes-and-harness** — verify logs its spawns at the call site, and `run_bounded` stays unlogged. Each verify `process-exit` carries `child_exit_status` and `duration_ms`. In CI, verify runs only against the fake agent (`tests/cli_verify.rs`, harness `boot` step 4, `stamped_home`), and the real-`claude` verify is local only. This is the call-site precedent behind the extract's guard: moving the PTY behind a `cfg(test)` double must not move the `verify-pty-probe` pair into the seam. The fake-agent verify is also where the six-pair acceptance criterion can be read without a live `claude` start.
- **2026-09-29-t15-07-57-wrap** — (registry migration, the Decisions Log lifted into the body) §6 Child / shell spawns now carries D-35 in force: `diag-line.v1.json` `$defs.subject.enum` closes `subject`, and verify's spawn exits carry `child_exit_status` and `duration_ms`. - **2026-10-05-real-cli-verify-probes** — added the `verify-pty-probe` subject and one spawn pair per interactive PTY run, Run A and then Run B, logged at the call site. Every `process-exit` carries `child_exit_status` and `duration_ms`, and no line holds row text, a prompt or a path.
