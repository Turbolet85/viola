## Patterns to follow
- Appended lines, never redrawn: the TTY-only issue line stays and the refusal is printed under it, column for column; with stdout piped the issue line is dropped and the outcome line alone carries the mirror (per layout-templates §Output structure — `viola send`).
- Streams by kind: a result (the `release --budget` line) goes to stdout; the issue line, the refusal and its hint go to stderr; the two are never mixed (per layout-templates §Component — Primary content block 2: refusal lines and the `unable` column, Streams).
- The machine view replaces the human one whole: under `--json` one typed document on stdout, no glyph, no stderr line, no hint text, the typed exit kept (per layout-templates §Component — Hero / signature output line, Machine view).
- The last printed line says what happened, to whom and where, with no terminator word; on a refusal the hint is that last line (per layout-templates §Component — Footer / terminator).
- New fields go into `--json` first and the human words stay stable: the override's end in the machine result (scope item 4, inferred) fits this rule, and the human line adds no field beyond the three the plan places (per layout-templates §Surface: cli IA notes, Output as a contract).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- No colour on the mirror or on the gate words: the refused send is brackets and words only, and colour on the cli is reserved for the `DIALOG` word and a `stale` row; no spinner, tick or `done` (per layout-templates §Component — Hero / signature output line).
- No human surface for `viola hook`: the statusline arm's refusal (scope item 8), whatever the measurement folds, prints no line of its own on any stream (per layout-templates §Component — Header / banner (the BAY context line)).
- No new human column, cell or header in this chunk: the board's WHEEL suffix and the BAY line's gate cell are placed by layout-templates §Output structure — `viola list` (human TTY) and owned by another entry; this chunk writes the snapshot fields they read and draws neither.
