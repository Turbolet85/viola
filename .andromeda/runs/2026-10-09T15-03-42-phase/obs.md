## Constraints
- **`text_bytes` is the typed text's length, and the plan words the typed text as "the sent text without its trailing LF characters" in three passages**: the `pty.paste_write` span attribute and the `send-issued` field (per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback), and the `send-*` row (per obs-plan §6 Additive field catalog). The founder's ruling (scope §6) widens the strip to trailing CR and CRLF, so all three passages are stale once the strip lands. obs-plan requires `text_bytes` to equal the length of what is typed; whether every `text_bytes` call site reads the output of `typed_text` (and so follows the new strip with no edit) is research's question. The three re-wordings are an obs-plan amendment owed with security-plan §Input Validation's, at the chunk's wrap (phase amends no master).
- **Refusal details are a closed list taken from the arch `RefusalReason` verbatim** (per obs-plan §6 `detail` code catalog). The newline-only refusal's new detail is therefore named in arch first and mirrored in obs, never coined at a log call site. obs-plan enumerates the send details in four places that each need the new value once its name and side are decided: §1 Critical paths (Confirmed `send`, Required log fields), §1 Telemetry triggers (logging-sensitive, Vector 2), §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (the wrapper list `input-not-ready|no-prompt-submitted|turn-running` and the client-side validation line), and §6 `detail` code catalog. Whether `schemas/diag-line.v1.json` closes `send-refused.detail` to an enum (the plan states that only for `parse-rejected.detail`, D-31) is research's question; if it does, the schema moves with the detail or G4 reds.
- **The side that decides the refusal fixes the log shape** (per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback): a client-side validation refusal is `send-refused` with `corr` null, `side:"client"`, no `conn` / `rpc_id`, plus a wrapper mirror line if the frame reached it; a wrapper-side refusal carries `side:"wrapper"`, `wheel`, `conn` (or `srv_conn`) and `rpc_id`. obs-plan §1 Telemetry triggers (logging-sensitive, Vector 2) requires the validation refusal at both client and wrapper, without the text. The plan holds no row for a newline-only refusal: which of the two shapes it takes is the P4 contract decision scope §6 already names, and obs follows it.
- **A send refused before `send-issued` has no arch cursor** (per obs-plan §3 Log format JSON schema): its wrapper-side `send-refused` carries `corr` = the target's `events.ndjson` end offset at refusal, a log join key only, and the schema requires `corr` on every `send-*` line with `side:"wrapper"`. "Refused at once" (scope §6) puts the newline-only refusal in this class if the wrapper decides it: no `send-issued`, no `pty.paste_write`, no `run.confirm_window` precede it. The present outcome's line (`no-prompt-submitted` at window expiry, per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send`, Cleanup) is the one the ruling replaces for this input.
- **Refusals are normal product outcomes and log at `info`** (per obs-plan §6 Log levels mapping); a `parse-rejected` line is `warn`. Whether the newline-only refusal also writes `parse-rejected{parser:"paste-text"}` is not stated by the plan: that event's `detail` list is closed in the schema (per obs-plan §6 `detail` code catalog, D-31), so a new `paste-text` detail is a second catalog-plus-schema change, to be planned or explicitly not made.
- **Integration homes stay inside the gate scope** (per obs-plan §9 Pipeline integration, Integration tests row): every fake-agent run's viola home lives under `target/e2e-home/`, so G2, G4, the secret scan and the `diag-<os>` upload read it; the single carve-out is `tests/chaos_feed_panic.rs`'s `TestHome::outside_scan()`. Scope §1's lifted wrapper-boot helper in `tests/support/` must build homes the same way for every caller, moving no home out of scope and not pulling the chaos home in. Whether each per-file copy today roots its home there is research's question.
- **Obs code is mutated like product code, and a surviving obs-code mutant is a build failure at a `run --mutants`** (per obs-plan §9 Pipeline integration, Mutation row; obs-plan §10 Build / deploy failure conditions). Whether any of scope §5's eighteen survivors sits on an `obs_event!` call site or its guard (the `NotFound` guards in `viola-state`, `src/cmd/hook.rs:214:72`, `src/cmd/mod.rs:133:5` are the candidates by location) is research's question; one that does is killed by an assertion on the emitted line, not disposed by argument.

## Patterns to follow
- Emit only through `obs_event!` with explicit typed fields under `#[instrument(skip_all, fields(..))]`; a helper split out of `record` or `dialog_variants` (scope §2) that gains a span takes `skip_all` as its first argument (per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing → Integration points 1; obs-plan §9 Gate commands G1).
- Keep verify's spawn logging at the call site when `record` is split: the six `process-start` / `process-exit` pairs stay where they are and `run_bounded` stays unlogged (per obs-plan §6 Boundary-call wrappers, Child / shell spawns). "No behaviour changes" (scope §2) includes the emitted lines, their order and their fields.
- A test that pins the new refusal or the strip reads process logs by the closed fields (`event`, `refusal`, `detail`, `side`, `text_bytes`) with `jq`-style selection, and treats an absent `corr` as null (per obs-plan §3 Log format JSON schema, Null encoding).
- New test inputs for the CR, CRLF and newline-only cases are synthetic and carry the tests-owned canary where they carry content, so the home-level scan can assert its absence (per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing → Integration points 6).
- The new detail is one more closed kebab-case value, so the derived send counters keep grouping by `side` and `detail` with no unbounded key (per obs-plan §5 Metric Coverage).

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Never log the sent text, a fragment of it, or which characters it held, in any home-level line: the newline-only refusal and the CR/CRLF sends log codes and `text_bytes` only (per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns § PII Scrubbing).
- Never add an `event` value or a field outside the catalog to carry the new outcome: it rides `send-refused` with the existing `refusal` / `detail` / `side` fields (per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns § Logs; default-deny per obs-plan §8 Default-deny posture).
- Never move a red G2 / G4 / secret-scan to green by taking a home out of `target/e2e-home/` or by a re-run while the scaffolding is lifted (per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns § CI; obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns § SLO).

## Acceptance criteria contributions
- A newline-only `send` leaves exactly one `send-refused` line per deciding side, carrying the closed `refusal` and the new `detail`, the `side`, no text and no `text_bytes` of a paste, at `INFO`, with no `send-issued` or `send-confirmed` for that call; on a wrapper-side line `corr`, `conn` (or `srv_conn`) and `rpc_id` are present (per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback; obs-plan §6 Log levels mapping).

- G2, G4 and the secret scan read green over the homes the lifted `tests/support/` scaffolding creates, with zero schema-conformance failures on lines carrying the new detail, and the home count under `target/e2e-home/` not reduced by the lift (per obs-plan §9 Gate commands; obs-plan §10 Error budget).
