# obs extract

## Relevance
partial — the chunk is mostly about bounding and validating inputs, which is security's ground. Obs owns how each rejected input is recorded: the closed `parse-rejected` / `send-refused` / `hook-decision` lines, the codes-only floor, the vt100 degrade's one-line-per-episode rule and G4 schema conformance. The chunk adds no obs infrastructure.

## Constraints
- A bounded reader that rejects its input is recorded as `parse-rejected{parser, detail, count}`. `parser` must be one of the closed nine (`claude-agents-json|vt100-feed|config-json|ledger-stamps|hook-stdin|channel-frame|prompt-submitted|paste-text|state-entry`), and `detail` must come from that parser's closed list (`malformed`, `unreadable`, `oversize`, `unknown-keys`, `panicked`, `control-character`, `symlink-ignored`, `strict-modes-failed`), per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage (the `parse-rejected` row and the `parse-rejected` detail catalog, D-05 / D-31). Some readers the scope newly caps have no `parser` value today: `claude --version`, `statusline-source.json`, `settings.json`, `snapshot.json`, `events.ndjson` and the status-line stdout. Whether each of them should write a line at all, or none, is research's question. Adding a value needs a Decisions Log entry; it is never improvised.
- `budget.json` is an exception. A refused, unreadable, over-cap or other-`v` `budget.json` counts as no reading, with no span and no line, per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Budget governor. A cap added there must keep that silence.
- A rejected hook stdin (oversize or malformed) writes `parse-rejected{parser:"hook-stdin", detail:"oversize"|"malformed", count:1}`, then the fail-open `hook-decision{detail:"oversize-stdin"|"malformed-json"}` at WARN. The hook then exits 0 with stderr empty, all per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage (`hook-decision` fail-open codes). A `channel-frame` beyond `MAX_FRAME` is `detail:"oversize"`, per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage.
- The paste rule has its own telemetry: one `send-refused{refusal:"not-delivered", detail:"control-character"|"empty-text"}` per deciding side, and never the text. On the client it is `side:"client"` with `corr:null`, written before any frame. On the wrapper it is `side:"wrapper"`, carrying `conn` and `rpc_id` and no `send-issued`. All per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback. Only `text_bytes` (the typed text's length) may describe the text, per the same §4 Scenario.
- A caught vt100 panic is recorded once per poisoning episode: exactly one `parse-rejected{parser:"vt100-feed", count:1}` at WARN with no `corr`, `detail` `panicked` (or `oversize` for a copy the bounded feed queue dropped), alongside the panic hook's one `event:"panic"` role line. `run` continues and nothing is sent to `main`, per obs-plan §7 Error Capture & Reporting (vt100 bullet and `run` worker threads). `run.readiness_gate` records `outcome` (`ready|input-not-ready`) and `vt100_panicked`, per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback.
- Codes only: no rejected input byte, entry name, link target, path, pid or upstream text reaches a home-level line or a `detail` code. Channel lines never carry `params` or result bodies, and an MCP `invalid-params` `detail` is a closed field word (`target · text · after · timeout_ms · dialog_id · response`), all per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing and obs-plan §1 Obs Scope Summary (telemetry triggers, Vectors 1, 2 and 5).
- A name that reaches a log field is validated as well. `from` is logged only when it is a valid `ViolaName`. `scanned` is the instance directory's last component only when it parses as a `ViolaName`, and otherwise absent. Per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage (the `release-from-driver` and `state-recovered` rows). Unknown event kinds, unknown fields and torn lines are skipped and counted (`skipped`), per obs-plan §1 Obs Scope Summary (telemetry triggers, Parser surfaces).

## Patterns to follow
- Every boundary line goes through `obs_event!` under `#[instrument(skip_all, name = "...", fields(...))]` with an explicit typed allow-list, per obs-plan §4 Span / Trace Coverage and obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns (Logs).
- Hook stdin rejections use `reject_stdin` in `src/cmd/hook.rs`, which writes the `hook-stdin` line and then fails open, per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Budget governor.
- A scan that skips entries counts them and writes one line per nonzero count after its loop, naming no entry. The model is `viola_state::board::read`, which writes `parse-rejected{parser:"state-entry", detail:"symlink-ignored"|"malformed", count}`, per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage (Filesystem refusals).
- A per-key config fallback is the `read_thresholds` shape: read through `take(MAX_FRAME)`, one `parse-rejected{parser:"config-json"}` per bad key, and the key's default taken, per obs-plan §3 OTel SDK init (init step 6).
- `tests/chaos_feed_panic.rs` is the run-level witness pattern for the vt100 degrade: one `parse-rejected{vt100-feed, panicked}`, one `event:"panic"`, a `send` refused with `input-not-ready`, and a typed key still reaching the child. Its home sits outside `target/e2e-home` (`TestHome::outside_scan()`), per obs-plan §7 Error Capture & Reporting.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- No per-byte or per-chunk span or event on the PTY pump or the vt100 feed while bounding them. Log the gate or episode outcome and counts only, per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns (Telemetry Strategy; Logs).
- No new `event` value, `parser` / `detail` value or field outside the §6 catalog without a Decisions Log entry. Default-deny is enforced by G4, per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns (Logs; PII Scrubbing) and obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing (default-deny posture).
- No path, pid, rejected content or type name inside a `detail` code or an error-bearing span field, and no `#[instrument(err)]` / `#[instrument(ret)]` on a parse whose error can carry a serde source, per obs-plan §11 Obs Anti-Patterns (Spans / Traces; PII Scrubbing).

## Contract bindings
- obs ↔ tests: `schemas/diag-line.v1.json` closes the `parse-rejected` `parser` and `detail` values (D-31) and the `process-exit` `detail` enum, and it is validated by G4 and `tests/contract_diag_schema.rs`. Any line a new bound writes must conform, per obs-plan §3 Log format JSON schema (bound to test-plan §3 Log format) and obs-plan §9 CI Integration (G4).
- obs ↔ security: two rules are security-plan's (§Input Validation), and obs logs their outcomes codes-only under the NEVER-log floor. The first is the paste rule and `validate_paste_text`. The second is `take(MAX_FRAME)` plus serde_json default depth. Per obs-plan §8 PII Scrubbing.
- obs ↔ tests: `tests/chaos_feed_panic.rs` sits outside the G2 / G4 / secret-scan scope by the founder's ruling. A new vt100-degrade witness either joins it or keeps its home under `target/e2e-home/`, where G2 counts its panic, per obs-plan §9 CI Integration (pipeline integration) and obs-plan §7 Error Capture & Reporting.

## Acceptance criteria contributions
- Each boundary witness that rejects an oversize or malformed input asserts two things. First, any line the plan names for that reader carries only closed `parser` / `detail` / `count` (or the closed refusal pair). Second, the rejected bytes appear in no home-level `diagnostics/*.ndjson` (per obs-plan §6 Log Coverage and §8 PII Scrubbing).
- A paste refused on the client gives exactly one `send-refused{side:"client", detail:"control-character"|"empty-text"}` and no frame. A paste refused on the wrapper, from a frame sent straight to it, gives exactly one `send-refused{side:"wrapper"}` with `conn` and `rpc_id` and no `send-issued`. Neither line carries the text (per obs-plan §4 Scenario: Confirmed `send` (CL-1) from driver to readback).
- A vt100 panic gives exactly one `parse-rejected{parser:"vt100-feed", detail:"panicked"}` and one `event:"panic"` line. After it, `send` is refused `input-not-ready` (exit 13) while a human key still reaches the child (per obs-plan §7 Error Capture & Reporting).
- G4 reports zero schema-conformance failures over every line the chunk's tests produce, and zero G2 panics outside the seam exemption (per obs-plan §10 SLO Invariants & Telemetry Budgets, error budget).
