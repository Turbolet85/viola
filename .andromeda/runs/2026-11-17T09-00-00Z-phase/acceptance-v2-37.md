After a test process that booted a wrapper on pipes, by a direct spawn, under an outer PTY, frozen under an outer PTY, or under an in-test harness session is SIGKILLed mid-run, every process of that test's recorded tree (the wrapper, its fake agent, the supervisor) is gone by pid and start time within WITHIN (killed_test_leaves_no_{piped,direct,outer_pty,frozen}_wrapper in tests/cross_killed_test.rs, killed_test_leaves_no_harness_session in crates/viola-e2e/tests/harness_tied.rs); a test that panics leaves none (panicked_test_leaves_no_piped_wrapper); no root test spawns viola outside tests/support/tied.rs (cross_killed_test_census_finds_no_untied_viola_spawn); a test timed out is one the runner kills, the case the witnesses drive, and a test that passes or fails ends its wrapper through the existing Drop guards.
