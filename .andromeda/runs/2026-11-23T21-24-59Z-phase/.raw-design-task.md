@task_prose part=chunk
Adds a new flat CLI verb `viola open`: starts a terminal window per session beside its parent, foot started directly with a closed flag allowlist and no shell, then `viola run` inside it.
Places the new window with the seam's one closed move dispatch to the parent's workspace (or --workspace N), records unplaced or unsupported when placement is skipped, and returns once the instance is live (30 s bound) or exits 1 not-live.
The wrapper records its own window: a new log-only event kind `window` and a snapshot `window?`, origin (cli or agent) handed through one hidden checked flag on `viola run`; `list --json` gains window?/origin? while the six-column board stays unchanged.
Adds tests for the recorder's three untested record-move failure codes (move-refused, move-unseen, window-left) over the planted stubs, and lands the 5 s compositor read deadline as a time-bound row of its own.
Re-measures the seam's counts at HEAD and re-reads the prior split-carry's reached rows; stops before the one live reading R2.
