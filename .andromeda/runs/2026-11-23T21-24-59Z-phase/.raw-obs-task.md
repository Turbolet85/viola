@task_prose part=chunk
Builds the verb `viola open`: one terminal window (foot, started directly, no shell) per session beside its parent, behind a closed flag allowlist, placed on the parent's workspace through the Hyprland seam's one move dispatch, returning once the new instance is live.
Adds the window record: the wrapper (`viola run`) records its own window and the origin open hands it (cli or agent) as a new log-only event kind `window`, a snapshot field `window?` replayed last-line-wins, and `window?` / `origin?` on `list --json`; logged codes-only.
Adds tests for the hypr-tap recorder's three untested record-move failure codes (move-refused, move-unseen, window-left) over planted stubs, and lands the 5 s compositor read bound (HYPRCTL_DEADLINE) and open's new bounds as time-bound rows of their own.
Re-measures at HEAD every seam count carried from the cancelled take-up's split-carry and re-reads its reached rows; the one live reading (R2, an open beside a live parent under a scratch home) is never run in CI and the implement step stops before it.
