Adds tests for the Hyprland recorder's three untested record-move failure codes over planted stubs, and joins the 5 s compositor read deadline to the time-bound rows.
Re-measures the earlier split carry's seam counts and re-reads its reached rows at HEAD; the one live end-to-end reading (R2) is the stop and is not run here.

@task_item n=4 part=needs
