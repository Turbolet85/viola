  > `<hash>` is a truncated SHA-256 of the exe bytes (the first 16 hex characters of the digest). The SHA-256 implementation is `sha2 =0.11.0` with `default-features = false` (Decisions Log `2026-09-25`): pure Rust, no `cc` on any of the three `[graph]` triples, passing the cargo-deny C-build ban; `tests/contract_content_hash.rs` pins it against published FIPS 180-2 vectors and the 16-hex truncation. A non-cryptographic fallback is banned (§ Data Protection). Because the hash is cryptographic, this catches substitution as well as accidental corruption. Tampering by the same OS user (who could also rewrite the folder name) stays outside the v1 boundary.
