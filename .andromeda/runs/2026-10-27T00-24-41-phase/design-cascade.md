## Bindings
- **expression-motion ↔ cli-output**: the 0.0 passthrough row forbids any byte, glyph or spinner while `viola run` or a passed `viola revive` holds the terminal (.andromeda/design-system-detail/brand-identity.md:21), and the colour decision order puts `viola run` and `viola hook` ahead of every styling choice (.andromeda/design-system.md:246); together they bound what a pin wait or retry at the start, and the agent's hook spawn of the pinned path, may print: nothing.
- **cli-output ↔ cli-send ↔ cli-exit-codes**: a pin refusal takes the fixed `unable:` form with its hint (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:21), joins `run`'s per-cause list (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:65) and the exit-1 row (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:141) in that same wording, so a cause added in one place is added in all three.
- **cli-verify-run ↔ cli-exit-codes**: `revive`'s start reaching the pin write prints no `--json` document for a `run` start refusal (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:132), the same gap the exit-1 row's `--json` cell records (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:141); `verify`'s pin arm keeps the step counter its one progress pattern (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:113), and any refusal it gains is listed among `verify`'s exit-1 refusals (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:141).
- **expression-motion ↔ cli-verify-run**: the human TTY row's no-spinner, no-redraw rule (.andromeda/design-system-detail/brand-identity.md:20) and the step counter as the one progress pattern (.andromeda/design-system-detail/surface.md:113) together forbid a pin wait in `verify` from showing progress of its own.
