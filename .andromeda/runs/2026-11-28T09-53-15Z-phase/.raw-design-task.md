@task_prose part=chunk
Builds the view of viola console: the frame past the scaffold's one fixed frame, drawing the home's instances as lineage trees.
Each row shows a session's state, wheel holder and last activity in the board's own words, read from the board read model.
Every drawn string is control-escaped, and the frame re-reads the fleet so a change on disk shows without a key.
The frame stays whole (no cut mid-row, no wrap) at a quarter-screen tile; the compositor half is a live reading only, never fired here.
It reuses the six board captions with their words and slot order, and keeps the scaffold's keys and refusals as they are.
