@task_prose part=chunk
Builds the view `viola console` draws past the scaffold's one fixed frame: the home's instances as lineage trees in the board's own lineage order, each session's state, wheel holder and last activity in the board's words, the six board captions reused with their words and slot order.
Every upstream- or disk-sourced string the view draws passes the existing control escaping, so no raw C0, C1 or DEL byte reaches the terminal.
The frame stays current without a key: it re-reads the fleet so a new instance, a state change, a wheel move or an exit shows; the mechanism and its cost are measured on fleets of 1, 8 and 33 test-owned instances.
The frame stays whole (no row cut, no wrap) at a quarter-screen tile's size, with a rule for a fleet or a row that does not fit; the compositor half is a live reading left to the operator, never fired here.
Adds no action, key, send or selection, and starts nothing live: no window, no compositor dispatch, no real claude, no read of the operator's real home.
