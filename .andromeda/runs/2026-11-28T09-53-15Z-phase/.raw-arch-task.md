@task_prose part=chunk
Builds the view of `viola console`, past the scaffold's one fixed frame: the fleet's lineage trees, each session's state, wheel holder and last activity in the board's words, every drawn string control-escaped.
The frame re-reads the fleet so a change on disk (a new instance, a state change, a wheel move, an exit) shows without a key; the mechanism and its cost are measured on fleets of 1, 8 and 33 test-owned instances.
The frame stays whole at the size a quarter-screen tile gives, nothing cut mid-row and no wrap, with a defined answer for a fleet or row that does not fit.
It reuses the six board captions with their words and slot order; it adds no action, key, send or selection, and fires no compositor reading.

@task_item n=3 part=needs

@task_item n=7 part=needs

@task_item n=8 part=needs
Kept current without a daemon: the disk is the shared truth and the console re-reads it the way `viola list` reads state, caller-side and without a wrapper or channel method; the mechanism (a re-read on the 100 ms tick, on a file change, or a subscription) and its cost are P3's measurement on 1, 8 and 33 test-owned instances.
@task_item n=9 part=needs
The console's read of the home obeys the board's read rules: the strict-modes read rule held as `viola list` holds it, the whole board refused on one failed check, the board read model sync and Claude-free.
