@task_prose part=chunk
Builds the view of `viola console`, past the scaffold's one fixed frame: the home's instances as lineage trees in the board's lineage order and indentation, each session's state, wheel holder and last activity in the board's words, every row control-escaped.
The frame is kept current: it re-reads the fleet so a new instance, a state change, a wheel move or an exit shows without a key (mechanism and cost measured at P3 on fleets of 1, 8 and 33 test-owned instances).
The frame stays whole at a quarter-screen tile size, nothing cut mid-row and no wrap, with a defined handling of a fleet or row that does not fit; the compositor half (opening tiled on the founder's host) is a live reading only, never fired here.
It reuses the six board captions with their words and slot order, adds no action, key, send or selection beyond the scaffold's, and stands against the verb-output rows as they stand while the founder's fork on them is open.
