(1) Linear and static: `wait_human_lines_are_linear` reads `waiting: <name>` exactly once on stderr under the outer PTY and none piped, and on Unix `viola wait`'s and `viola --help`'s terminal streams hold no ESC byte and no CR not followed by LF (`wait_human_lines_are_linear`, `help_is_plain_on_a_terminal`).
(2) No colour or glyphs under a non-TTY stdout, `NO_COLOR` (an empty value included), `TERM=dumb` or `--json`: `cross_list_rows_print_no_sgr_under_no_color_dumb_piped_or_json` (zero ESC on Unix, an SGR attribute set within {0} on windows-2025) and one `colour_decision_each_rung_switches_colour_off` case per rung.
(3) Every colour span beside its word: `cross_list_rows_style_each_span_beside_its_word_on_a_terminal` reads bold only around NAME values and the attention token only around the `DIALOG` word (its kind word plain), no dim on a live board, the depth by `COLORTERM` / `TERM`; the `board_row_style_*` units hold dim only on a stale row with the cocked composition (the `DIALOG` word amber and not dim), and `unable` / `fail` carry no SGR.
(4) Injected controls appear only escaped in `wait`, `last` and `list` output: the `last` case of `tests/cli_wait_last.rs`, `cross_list_rows_print_escaped_in_the_captions_columns_in_every_mode`, the `escape_*` units and `board_row_style_*` (g), which reads an escaped control the same at every depth.
(5) `viola verify` appends one `[NN/MM] … pass|fail` step line per relied row and the `stamped` summary: the `tests/cli_verify.rs` literals.
(6) Under `viola run` viola adds zero bytes to the terminal: the `tests/tui_passthrough.rs` cases (byte for byte against an unwrapped run on Unix, viola's literals absent on windows-2025).
(7) Human keys pass unblocked during a driver send: `send_under_the_paste_hint_a_human_key_during_the_gate_wait_wins` (the key reaches the child, the send exits 10 `human-typing` with nothing typed) and `paste_human_bytes_during_a_paste_land_wholly_after_it`.
